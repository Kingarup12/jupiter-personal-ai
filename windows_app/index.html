About Jupiter

Jupiter is a personal AI created by the project creator, designed to be more than just an assistant.

Built around trust, personalization, and meaningful interaction, Jupiter is designed to understand you better over time — your preferences, routines, conversations, and the things that matter to you.

The idea behind Jupiter is simple: to create a truly personal AI that feels familiar, reliable, private, and available whenever you need her.

From everyday conversations and questions to productivity, creativity, learning, and assistance, Jupiter is designed to adapt to the person using her rather than treating everyone the same.

Created by

the project creator

Jupiter began as the maintainer vision of building his own intelligent system — an AI centered around trust, personality, memory, and a more personal connection between people and technology.
